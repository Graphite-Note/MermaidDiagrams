`XYChartCorpusTests` Mermaid's 8 XY chart examples and generated charts; `SankeyCorpusTests` Mermaid's 8 Sankey examples, the spec's energy flows and generated flows; `KanbanParserTests`, `TreeViewParserTests`, `TreeViewBoxDrawingTests` and `IshikawaParserTests` port Mermaid 12.1.0's specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat`), and the layout tests read and lay out Mermaid's 3 kanban, 13 tree view and 1 Ishikawa examples and generated boards, trees and fishbones; `SimpleChartSafetyTests` runs Pele's worst cases for the four, cut and mutated examples, and a deep nest, and `DiagramTextFittingTests` covers the text fitting packets and treemaps share; `VennCorpusTests` Mermaid's 8 Venn examples (each region's text apart from the others'), Pele's worst cases and generated sets; `CynefinCorpusTests` Mermaid's 3 Cynefin examples (blocks apart and on the page), Pele's worst cases and generated frameworks; `WardleyMapCorpusTests` Mermaid's 19 Wardley examples (every text on the page), Pele's worst cases and generated maps; `EventModelCorpusTests` Mermaid's 12 event model examples, across and down a column; `RailroadCorpusTests` Mermaid's 14 railroad examples in the four notations, regular and tight, and generated grammars. `EventModelParserTests`, `RailroadParserTests` and `RailroadExpressionTests` port the inputs of Mermaid's specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/eventmodeling`, `tests/compat/railroad`) and Pele's own model tests; `TrackDiagramSafetyTests` runs Pele's worst cases for both types; `C4ParserTests` ports the inputs of Mermaid's C4 specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/c4`) and the model cases of Pele's render tests, `C4LayoutEngineTests`, `C4StyleAndPlacementTests` and `C4RoutingNumbersTests` its layout, routing, style and narrow-column cases (a loop and a detour in Pele's numbers worked out by hand, and every example's drawing kept value for value), `C4ScenePaintingTests` the scene's order and paints as Pele's SVG has them, and `C4CorpusTests` Mermaid's 6 C4 examples at their width and tight at 640, 480 and 320 points (every line clear of the shapes it does not join but the one Pele leaves too), Pele's worst cases and generated diagrams. `RequirementParserTests` ports the inputs of Mermaid's requirement specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/requirement`), `RequirementReadingTests` the lexer's quirks, the typed errors and the database's spec, `RequirementLayoutEngineTests` the layout in Pele's numbers and `RequirementCorpusTests` Mermaid's 9 requirement examples and generated diagrams; `LayeredCompoundLayoutTests`, `LayeredCompoundRoutingTests`, `LayeredCompoundGroupTests` and `LayeredCompoundTrackTests` port Pele's tests of its layered and compound layouts (`tests/layout/compound.test.ts`, `layered.test.ts`: 300 random graphs well formed, spreading, settling, levels, tracks, twins, ports, route crossings) and `LayeredLayoutWorstCaseTests` its worst cases for them (`tests/stress.test.ts`: chains and stars of 5,000, 2,000 edges each crossing 1,000 nested borders, a complete graph of 60, each within 3 seconds), and `LayeredSortingEquivalenceTests` that the layout's own sorts give the orders of those they replaced (the stable sort through a reused spare list, the middle two of four positions); `LaneLayoutTests` and `LaneLayoutRandomTests` port Pele's lane layout tests (`tests/layout/lanes.test.ts`), `SwimlaneLayoutEngineTests` the swimlane variant's drawing and `SwimlaneCorpusTests` Mermaid's 13 swimlane examples, regular and narrow; `LayeredDiagramSafetyTests` runs Pele's worst cases for both types. `UseCaseLexerTests`, `UseCaseGrammarTests`, `UseCaseDiagramTests`, `UseCaseContradictionTests`, `UseCaseJSONTests` and `UseCaseGraphTests` port the inputs of Mermaid's use case specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/usecase`, `tests/compat/usecase-parser`), `UseCaseLayoutEngineTests` the layout in Pele's numbers worked out by hand, `UseCaseSceneTests` the scene's order and paints, the preparation and Pele's worst cases (`tests/usecase/stress.test.ts`), and `UseCaseCorpusTests` Mermaid's 24 use case examples across and turned down. `PeleThemePaletteTests` ports Pele's palette tests (`tests/render/palette.test.ts`) and `DiagramSeriesColorsTests` resolves, paints, writes and inks theme palettes (CSS's color functions and mixing against known values, cycles, hostile values, the documentation examples with palettes). Setting `MERMAID_DIAGRAMS_PICTURES` to a folder makes the theme palette tests, the Venn, Cynefin, Wardley, C4, requirement, swimlane and use case corpus tests write their scenes there as PNG pictures to look at.
`BlockParserTests` ports the inputs of Mermaid's block specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/block`), `BlockLayoutEngineTests` the relations of Pele's block render tests and numbers worked out by hand, `BlockEdgeInkTests` the casings and inks of edges over filled composites as Pele draws them, `BlockCorpusTests` lays out Mermaid's 30 block examples, regular and tight, and generated grids (writing pictures when `MERMAID_DIAGRAMS_PICTURES` is set), and `GridDiagramSafetyTests` runs Pele's worst cases that fit the text limit. `ArchitectureParserTests` ports the inputs of Mermaid's architecture specs (`tests/compat/architecture`) and Pele's own rules, `ArchitectureLayoutEngineTests` the relations of its render tests, `ArchitectureCorpusTests` lays out Mermaid's 6 architecture examples, regular and tight, and generated architectures, and `GridDiagramSafetyTests` runs Pele's architecture worst cases too.
`AgentFlowReferenceTests` compares the model with what Mermaid 12.1.0 itself built for Pele's 239 reference inputs (the texts refused, nodes, edges, flows and class definitions with their metadata, the graph with its shapes, parents, palette slots, styles and curves, element positions and diagnostics; all but Mermaid's semantic model), `AgentFlowParserTests` and `AgentFlowDiagnosticsTests` port the inputs of Mermaid's agentflow specs as Pele ports them (`tests/compat/agentflow`) and Pele's stress shapes, `AgentFlowLayoutEngineTests` checks Pele's numbers worked out by hand and the scene's order and paints, and `AgentFlowCorpusTests` lays out Mermaid's 12 agent flow examples, across and turned down, and generated flows (writing pictures when `MERMAID_DIAGRAMS_PICTURES` is set).


`PeleDifferentialTests` compares, type by type, the scene of every input of Pele's corpora (Mermaid's documentation examples of every type, Pele's agent flow reference and the generated graphs of Pele's benchmark: 765 inputs, and 60 random flat flowcharts the runner generates for the layered layout) with what Pele 1.13.0 itself drew (`pele-corpus.json` and `pele-random-flat.json`, recorded by `scripts/pele/run.sh` in Deno's sandbox, Pele measuring text with its table of Arial's advances and the package in Arial): which inputs are refused, the page within 2 points or 1 %, the number of texts and of edges. Every input that differs is listed with its reason in `PeleKnownDifferences.md` (22 of 825 today: intended refusals and wordings, texts written in other pieces, text beyond ASCII and the monospaced font measured in the system's fonts, and text a class sets bold; none left untraced, and none is the layered layout's, which is Pele's), and the test fails when that list and the drawings disagree. `FlowchartSpecDifferentialTests` reads the 956 texts Mermaid's flowchart parser specs parse (`tests/compat/flowchart`) and compares each flowchart with Pele's database (direction, nodes, node texts, edges with their marks, strokes, lengths and texts, groups): all 956 agree, each of Pele's texts compared as its label layout draws it (`PeleDrawnText`). `PeleYAMLDifferentialTests`, `PelePreprocessingDifferentialTests` and `JavaScriptDateDifferentialTests` compare the package with Pele's own YAML reader, its preprocessing (directives within and across lines, tags' attributes, line breaks, front matter, colour semicolons) and V8's `new Date`, on texts `scripts/pele/run.sh --preprocessing` recorded (`pele-preprocessing.json`). `PeleNarrowDifferentialTests` compares the scene a column 280, 390 and 600 points wide shows of every documentation example with what Pele shows in a container that wide (`scripts/pele/run.sh --max-width=280,390,600`, `pele-narrow.json`), each difference listed in PeleKnownDifferences.md's `## Narrow columns`. `PeleCompatCoverageTests` walks the diagram texts of every spec file of Pele's `tests/compat` (`pele-compat-inputs.json`) and fails for a file of which none is found in the package's tests and resources unless it is listed with what ports it.
